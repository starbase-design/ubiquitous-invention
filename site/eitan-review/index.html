1The direction

A short documentary portrait: calm picture, quick story. The speed comes from the cutting and the narration. The look stays quiet.

  1. One look from start to finish.The cut now changes its look twelve times. Choose one way to show a name, one way to show a photo and one way to change scene, then repeat them.
  2. Real pictures first.His face, his office, the kippahs, the candle lighting. Add a graphic only when it tells the viewer something the picture cannot.
  3. Fast through cutting, not effects.A new shot every two or three seconds is fine for social. No zoom jumps, flashes, glitches or freeze frames. Nothing on screen for less than a second.
  4. Quiet text.One typeface, medium weight, normal upper and lower case. Giant capital words and stickers are what make it feel like a meme edit.
  5. The humour comes from him.His short dry answers are funny on their own. Hold the shot and let the pause work. No confetti, error box or dictionary card.
  6. One piece of music, almost no sound effects.Calm music that can be heard on a phone, and the quiet sound of the room under the pauses.
  7. Natural colour.No yellow, teal or navy backdrops. The rooms he is in are the colours of the film.

3What to keep

The calm film is already in the footage. These shots need nothing added.

  • The opening exchange. “Today is Friday. Let’s go party!” “I’m organizing Shabbat for 150 people.” It tells you who he is in a few seconds.
  • His dry answers. “Sort of.” “Getting used to it.” “Watching soccer.” “I also have two kids.”
  • The interview shots. Soft natural light, good framing, sharp.
  • Three real details. The kippahs at 0:55, the phone scene at 1:12, the candle lighting at 1:26.
  • The shape of the story. Who he is, where he came from, what he does, why he does it.
  • The last beat. He turns the question back: “Have you ever attended?”
  • The basics. Captions match the words and arrive on time. Overall loudness is right. Picture quality is clean.

4Order of work

  1. Take out the effects: cutouts, coloured backdrops, giant words, stickers, the rewind, confetti, the dictionary card, the error box, freeze frames, zoom jumps and sound effects.
  2. Rebuild the first minute with real pictures: the introduction (scene 2) and his past (scene 4).
  3. Set one caption style and one text style for the whole film.
  4. Put in new music and clean up the sound.
  5. Rewrite and re-record the narration lines.
  6. Replace the repeated and blurry photos.

8Technical checks

For the person who builds and exports the file. These are the numbers behind the fixes above.

Caption contrast
24 of 172 captions have less than 3:1 contrast with what is behind them. Yellow is worst: 11 of 38. Aim for 4.5:1 or more, which an outline or dark strip gives you.
Caption time on screen
Half are fully visible for 0.31 s or less, and 47 for four frames or fewer. Aim for at least half a second each.
Caption position
Now between 1095 and 1505 px from the top of the 1920 px frame. Keep text above about 1250 px: Instagram’s guidance keeps the bottom 35% clear and TikTok’s the bottom 660 px. Leave 65 to 120 px free at each side.
Captions in Remotion
If they come from createTikTokStyleCaptions, raise combineTokensWithinMilliseconds to 1200 or more to get phrases, and remove the fade.
Map border
Ukraine is drawn without Crimea from 0:33.5 to 0:37.7. This is the default in Natural Earth map data, which draws borders by who controls the ground. Use its version that includes Crimea in Ukraine, or leave the map out.
Skipped frame
In all live footage one frame in ten is skipped, which suggests the clips run at 110% speed. Play them at 100%, or retime them with frame blending first.
Colour settings
The file is tagged full-range BT.601 (yuvj420p, bt470bg). Platforms expect limited-range BT.709. Render with --color-space=bt709 and check that ffprobe reports yuv420p(tv, bt709). If it does not, convert once: 
ffmpeg -i in.mp4 -vf "scale=in_range=pc:out_range=tv:in_color_matrix=bt601:out_color_matrix=bt709,format=yuv420p" \
  -color_range tv -colorspace bt709 -color_primaries bt709 -color_trc bt709 \
  -c:v libx264 -crf 16 -preset slow -c:a copy out.mp4
Loudness
−14.6 LUFS overall, which is right. Peaks reach −0.7 dBTP. Set the limiter to −1.
